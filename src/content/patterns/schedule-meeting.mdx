## How it's composed

- **Calendar** owns date selection and keyboard navigation through the month.
- **Toggle group** with `orientation="vertical"` lists the open slots for the chosen day, one selectable at a time.
- **Button** repeats the full choice in its label, so confirming never needs a second look.

## Make it yours

Load the slots for the selected date, disable days with no availability, and wrap the slots under the Calendar on narrow screens.
