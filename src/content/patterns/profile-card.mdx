## How it's composed

- **Avatar** shows initials until a photo loads, and names the person for assistive technology.
- **Badge** pairs a role with a status without turning either into an action.
- **Button** gives follow the primary weight and message the secondary.

## Make it yours

Open this in a Preview card when people hover a name, or add a row of counts under the badges.
