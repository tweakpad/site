## How it's composed

- **Field** with `orientation="horizontal"` puts the label and description on one side and the Switch on the other, and clicking the label toggles it.
- **Switch** commits immediately, which suits settings that take effect without a save button.

## Make it yours

Save each change as it happens and confirm with a quiet toast, or add a Tabs row for Email, Push and In-app channels.
