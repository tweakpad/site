## How it's composed

- **Field** and **Native select** sit in one grid row, aligned on their bottom edge so the controls line up even though only one has a visible label above it.
- **List item** rows put the **Avatar** in `media` and the role **Badge** in `actions`; the Avatar's empty `alt` keeps the name from being read twice.
- **Button** offers the link as the secondary path and the invite as the primary one.

## Make it yours

Accept several addresses at once, or replace the Badge with a role select for admins.
