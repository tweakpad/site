## How it's composed

- **Slider** carries its own label and value, and supports arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, and Home and End.
- **Field** with a **Switch** ends the group with the one on and off setting.

## Make it yours

Add a mute Toggle beside each Slider, or show the level on a meter while audio plays.
