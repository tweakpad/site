## How it's composed

- **Toggle group** in single mode is the rating: one tab stop, arrow keys between options, and a visible pressed state.
- **Field** marks the comment as optional in its description instead of with an asterisk on the others.
- **Button** lets people dismiss the prompt as easily as they answer it.

## Make it yours

Ask the comment question only after a low rating, or replace the words with a 1 to 5 scale.
