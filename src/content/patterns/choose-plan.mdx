## How it's composed

- **Radio group** gives the three plans one tab stop with arrow-key navigation, and submits the chosen `value` with the form.
- **Badge** in the Card's `action` slot carries the yearly discount without competing with the title.
- **Button** spans the footer so the single next step is unmistakable.

## Make it yours

Add a Toggle group above the plans for monthly and yearly billing, and update the prices when it changes.
