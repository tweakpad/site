## How it's composed

- **Alert** explains the one-time reveal in the flow of the card, without interrupting with a dialog.
- **Input group** pairs a read-only **Input** with a **Copy button** in its `action` slot; the input keeps the key selectable for people who copy by hand.
- **Button** keeps rolling the key secondary to finishing.

## Make it yours

Confirm a roll with an Alert dialog, and mask the key after the first copy.
