The Audio Editor kit is a non-functional template you can extend: a session view composed from the foundation library plus a handful of controls that only make sense next to a timeline. It ships with a dark, low-glare theme tuned for long sessions.

The library's existing widgets, such as the curve editor and audio graph, are the starting point for these controls.
