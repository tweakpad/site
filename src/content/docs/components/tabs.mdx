## Usage

Author each tab as a native `<button slot="tab">` and each panel as an element with `slot="panel"`, matched by `value`. Tabs supplies roles, `type="button"`, roving tabindex and the `aria-controls` and `aria-labelledby` links; `label` names the tab list.

### Variants

<Preview
  title="Underline"
  align="stretch"
  code={`<tp-tabs variant="underline" default-value="overview" label="Project" style="max-inline-size:28rem;margin-inline:auto">
  <button slot="tab" value="overview">Overview</button>
  <button slot="tab" value="activity">Activity</button>
  <button slot="tab" value="settings">Settings</button>
  <p slot="panel" value="overview">Website redesign: 14 open tasks, due March 28.</p>
  <p slot="panel" value="activity">Jonas Weber moved “Pricing page” to Review 2 hours ago.</p>
  <p slot="panel" value="settings">Visibility, integrations and archive options.</p>
</tp-tabs>`}
/>

`enclosed` (the default) suits tabs that switch a contained surface; `underline` sits lighter on page-level navigation. The indicator glides to the selected tab in both.

### Keyboard and activation

<Preview
  title="Automatic activation"
  align="stretch"
  code={`<tp-tabs activation="automatic" default-value="day" label="Range" style="max-inline-size:24rem;margin-inline:auto">
  <button slot="tab" value="day">Day</button>
  <button slot="tab" value="week">Week</button>
  <button slot="tab" value="month">Month</button>
  <p slot="panel" value="day">1,204 visitors today.</p>
  <p slot="panel" value="week">8,931 visitors this week.</p>
  <p slot="panel" value="month">36,410 visitors this month.</p>
</tp-tabs>`}
/>

By default activation is `manual`: arrow keys move focus and Enter or Space selects. Use `automatic` when panels are cheap to show, so selection follows focus. Arrows wrap at the ends unless the `loopFocus` property is set to `false`.

### Disabled tabs

<Preview
  title="Disabled"
  align="stretch"
  code={`<tp-tabs default-value="details" label="Order" style="max-inline-size:26rem;margin-inline:auto">
  <button slot="tab" value="details">Details</button>
  <button slot="tab" value="shipping">Shipping</button>
  <button slot="tab" value="returns" aria-disabled="true">Returns</button>
  <p slot="panel" value="details">Order #10482, placed March 3.</p>
  <p slot="panel" value="shipping">Delivered March 6 by UPS.</p>
  <p slot="panel" value="returns">Returns open after delivery.</p>
</tp-tabs>`}
/>

`aria-disabled="true"` keeps a tab discoverable through arrow navigation; native `disabled` removes it from focus. Neither can be activated.

### Panel mounting

Inactive panels are detached from the DOM after their exit and restored on activation, so heavy content costs nothing while hidden. Add `keep-mounted` to a panel that must stay in the document, for example one holding form fields that still submit, and it is kept hidden and inert instead.
