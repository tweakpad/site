## Usage

`tp-bubble` renders one message; `tp-bubble-group` stacks consecutive messages from the same sender. Your application owns delivery, sender names and reaction counts; the Bubble only presents them.

### Treatments

<Preview
  title="Variants"
  align="stretch"
  code={`<div style="display:grid;gap:var(--tp-space-3);max-inline-size:32rem;margin-inline:auto">
  <tp-bubble variant="default">Default</tp-bubble>
  <tp-bubble>Secondary</tp-bubble>
  <tp-bubble variant="subdued">Subdued</tp-bubble>
  <tp-bubble variant="tinted">Tinted</tp-bubble>
  <tp-bubble variant="outline">Outline</tp-bubble>
  <tp-bubble variant="ghost">Ghost</tp-bubble>
  <tp-bubble variant="destructive">Message failed to send. Tap to retry.</tp-bubble>
</div>`}
/>

`secondary` is the default. Use one treatment per sender so the conversation reads at a glance, `ghost` for unframed assistant text that should take the full row, and `destructive` for delivery failures.

### Sender alignment

`align` is logical: `end` sits on the inline end and follows writing direction. Group consecutive messages so spacing reflects who is speaking, not each message.

<Preview
  title="Alignment"
  align="stretch"
  code={`<div style="display:grid;gap:var(--tp-space-6);max-inline-size:32rem;margin-inline:auto">
  <tp-bubble-group>
    <tp-bubble variant="subdued">Deploy finished for web-prod.</tp-bubble>
    <tp-bubble variant="subdued">Error rate is back under 0.1%.</tp-bubble>
  </tp-bubble-group>
  <tp-bubble-group>
    <tp-bubble align="end" variant="default">Thanks. I'll close the incident.</tp-bubble>
  </tp-bubble-group>
</div>`}
/>

### Reactions

The `reactions` slot accepts plain counts or real controls. `reaction-side` picks the top or bottom edge and `reactions-align` the inline edge; the two are independent. Give every reaction an accessible name.

<Preview

    <span slot="reactions" role="img" aria-label="Reactions: party popper and fire">🎉 🔥</span>

  <tp-bubble>
    Can someone own the follow-up survey?
    <tp-button slot="reactions" size="xs" variant="outline" aria-label="Like this message; 3 likes">Like · 3</tp-button>
  </tp-bubble>
</div>`}
/>

### Rich content

The default slot takes any content: paragraphs and links keep their own semantics. The message container is a named group (`label`, default "Message"); `label=""` removes the role.
